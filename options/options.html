Cross-Store Owned

Games you already own on the other store get a badge with that store's icon, like on Steam. Settings save as you change them.

Site access needed

Firefox has not given this extension access to Steam, GOG and IsThereAnyDeal, so it cannot read your libraries.

    Matching

    Keys are kept in this extension's local storage in Firefox, unencrypted, and only sent to the service they belong to. Empty a field to forget a key.

      Upkeep

      1 hour to 4 weeks

      Also on Firefox start-up and whenever you click Refresh now. Only new games are looked up again, so a refresh is quick after the first one.

      Hidden badges

      No badges are hidden. To hide a wrong match, open the toolbar popup on that store page and click Hide next to the game.
