Cross-Store Owned

Firefox has not given this extension access to Steam, GOG and IsThereAnyDeal, so it cannot read your libraries.

Games you own on one store get a badge on the other. Three steps to start:

  1. 1Log in to Steam in FirefoxOpen Steam
  2. 2Log in to GOG in FirefoxOpen GOG
  3. 3Read your libraries

Each step gets a ✓ once a refresh has read that store. The first read takes a few seconds per thousand games.
